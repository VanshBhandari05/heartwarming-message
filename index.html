Dear Rajasthan Royals,

I grew up in Jaipur watching Rajasthan Royals, so this round felt like a childhood dream coming true. I spent a lot of late nights on it and enjoyed every one.

The ball-by-ball data taught me how much a single pressure ball can say about a player. It also showed me how often my feelings about a player colour what I think I see. A name I love or doubt can quietly tilt my judgment, but the numbers don’t care. That is why I rank players by their floor, the worst realistic case, and not their best day.

Thank you for letting a fan from Jaipur look at the game this closely.

Halla Bol! 💗

Vansh Bhandari
